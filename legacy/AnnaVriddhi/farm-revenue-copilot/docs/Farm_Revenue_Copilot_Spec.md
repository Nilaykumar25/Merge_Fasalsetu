## Problem Statement
Small and marginal farmers lack timely, actionable guidance to maximise revenue from their crops. Decisions around irrigation, fertiliser, grading, and government scheme uptake are made without data — leaving significant income on the table.

## Solution
Farm Revenue Copilot is a mobile-first web application that:
1. Monitors crop health in near-real-time via sensors and photo capture
2. Delivers prioritised, plain-language recommendations (irrigation, fertiliser, harvest timing)
3. Matches farmers to eligible government and bank schemes
4. Provides an end-of-season revenue review with next-season planning

## Users
- **Primary:** Small/marginal farmer (≤5 acres), low digital literacy, feature-phone or entry Android
- **Secondary:** Extension worker / agri-input retailer advising multiple farmers

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Recommendation action rate | ≥ 60% within 48 h |
| Scheme uptake lift | +2 schemes per farmer per season |
| Net revenue improvement | +15% YoY |

## Out of Scope (v1)
- Marketplace / e-commerce
- Peer-to-peer farmer network
- Livestock management
